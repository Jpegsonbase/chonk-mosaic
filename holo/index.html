How it works

Each trait adds to Power, Guard, Speed and Swag by its slot (shoes are fast, tops are tough, accessories hit hard) and by what's in its name (a sword hits, a crown shines). Type comes from the Chonk's colours. HP grows with size and outfit. Head, Face and Accessory traits are the rare ones, so they set the rarity.
